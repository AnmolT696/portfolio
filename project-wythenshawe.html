Project Summary

I commenced the project by conducting detailed inspections of Reinforced Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (RAAC) planks across the Wythenshawe Hospital Maternity Unit, recording defects and escalating immediate structural concerns, before implementing short-term remedial works. I completed these inspections and remedial interventions across all affected areas, organised into four distinct phases.

To facilitate the phased decanting of clinical areas and enable remedial works to proceed, I designed additional extensions and new temporary structures, comprising a steel-framed corridor with timber floors and roofs on concrete strip and pad foundations, along with raft foundations for modular clinical units. I also designed the external works and below-ground drainage infrastructure required to serve the new structures.

Following completion of the external works, I extended the scope to roof replacement for Phase 3, coordinating with the wider design team — including architects and M&E consultants — and leading internal demolition design, temporary works, weekly coordination meetings, drainage proposals, regular site visits, and review of subcontractor drawings. External works additionally included new road enclosures and the widening of a private access road to a two-way route.

I also developed a concept design for a new steel frame extension to connect two separate corridors located in distinct buildings, in a restricted location surrounded by three existing structures with significant below-ground services. I undertook a feasibility study to define the scope of ground investigation requirements and produced two conceptual framing and foundation layouts as initial proposals for client discussion.
