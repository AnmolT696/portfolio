Summary

My involvement with the Institution of Structural Engineers goes beyond membership — it is an active commitment to the profession and to the development of the next generation of structural engineers across the Northwest of England.

As Chair of the Northwest Young Members Group since January 2026, I lead a regional committee that organises professional development events, site visits to live construction projects, technical presentations, and networking opportunities for early-career engineers. This role involves chairing monthly meetings, setting the strategic agenda, and maintaining strong engagement with IStructE's national leadership.

In 2024 I delivered a technical presentation at the IStructE Young Members Presentation event, which was recognised with a prize from Mohamed Al-Dah, President of the Institution, and an invitation to the IStructE Hot Pot Supper — a prestigious gathering of the institution's senior membership and leadership.

I have also organised site visits for the Northwest YMG group, including a visit to the Withington CDC project, giving members direct exposure to live composite steel frame construction at Stage 4–5.
