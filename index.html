About

Add photo → anmol-professional.jpg

I am a Graduate Structural Engineer (GIStructE) with over two years of UK consultancy experience at CCS Consulting, Manchester, where I have delivered structural designs from concept to construction across NHS healthcare, commercial, and residential sectors.

My work spans concrete, steel, timber, and masonry design to BS-EN Eurocodes and British Standards, with specialist experience in RAAC survey and remediation across live NHS hospitals in North West England — one of the most technically and logistically demanding environments in the industry.

Beyond engineering design, I develop Python automation tools to streamline workflows, and I actively contribute to the profession as Chair of the IStructE Northwest Young Members Group and as a CROSS UK Safety Report volunteer.

I am actively progressing toward MIStructE chartership and am open to roles that combine structural engineering rigour with innovative, high-impact projects.
